05 / EVALUATION FRAMEWORK

A controlled comparison of initializations.

Frozen κF

Load pretrained weights, freeze the encoder, and train the downstream classification head.

Fine-tuned κT

Load pretrained weights and train the encoder and downstream classification head.

Scratch κR

Train the same architecture and classification head from random initialization.

PRR Representation readiness

PRR = κF / κT

Values near 1 indicate little loss from freezing. Values above 1 mean the frozen setting outperforms fine-tuning.

ACS Relative scratch performance

ACS = κR / κT

Lower values indicate a larger relative gain from pretrained initialization. ACS ≥ 1 indicates no positive fine-tuning advantage over scratch.

PVS Joint positive value

PVS = 2 PRR (1 − ACS)PRR + 1 − ACS

For ACS < 1. Otherwise PVS = 0. The harmonic mean balances readiness and the relative gain, 1 − ACS.

Calculation and interpretation notes

Chance reference. Cohen’s κ has a chance reference of 0. Ratios are interpreted for κT > 0; PVS additionally requires PRR ≥ 0. Undefined scores remain unavailable rather than being replaced by zero.

Aggregation. Scores are ratios of the reported mean κ values, not means of subject-level ratios. Rankings use full precision; displayed values are rounded to four decimals. No pooled cross-dataset ranking is imposed.

Scope. ACS is a same-architecture training reference, not a causal decomposition of architecture and pretraining. PVS = 0 means the positive-gain criterion is not met for fine-tuning versus scratch; useful frozen representations can still exist.

Protocol and uncertainty. Results use leave-one-subject-out evaluation as supplied. The workbooks report mean ± values without defining the dispersion statistic; the table preserves them without labeling them as standard deviations or confidence intervals. No significance tests or checkpoint-version claims are inferred.
